Biography

Current position

Mauro Franceschelli is an Associate Professor of Automatic Control (IINF-04) at the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (DIEE), University of Cagliari, Italy, since July 2022.

His research projects are listed on the Projects page; the Ph.D. students and postdoctoral researchers he supervises or has supervised are listed on the Team page.

Research interests

His research focuses on cooperative distributed control of multi-agent systems and networks, with applications to multi-robot systems, distributed electric demand side management and response, and federated and distributed learning. His research interests include:

  • Distributed algorithms for coordination, control, estimation, optimization, consensus and learning in large groups of autonomous systems
  • Gossip-based algorithms for load balancing and task assignment
  • Resilient networks, resilient control and optimization; learning for control
  • Nonsmooth analysis and monotone systems
  • Applications to multi-robot systems and UAV coordination, sensor networks, smart grids and smart urban environments
